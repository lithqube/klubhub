# Design System Specification: High-Energy Cyberpunk Editorial

## 1. Overview & Creative North Star
**Creative North Star: "The Kinetic HUD"**
This design system rejects the static "website" feel in favor of a high-performance, tactical interface. Inspired by Heads-Up Displays (HUDs) and high-end editorial tech-wear catalogs, it prioritizes motion, data-density without clutter, and an unapologetic digital-first aesthetic.

We break the "template" look through **Intentional Asymmetry**. Containers are rarely perfectly centered; they lean into the edges of the screen. We use **Overlapping Elements**—such as headlines bleeding over the edges of glass containers—to create a sense of depth and urgency. This is not just a UI; it is an activated environment.

---

## 2. Colors & Surface Logic
The palette is rooted in the high contrast between the void of `surface` (#0E0E0F) and the radioactive energy of our accent tokens.

### The "No-Line" Rule
Traditional 1px solid borders are strictly prohibited for sectioning. To define boundaries, we use **Tonal Transitions** or **Luminous Thresholds**.
- **Sectioning:** Instead of a line, transition from `surface` to `surface_container_low`.
- **Luminous Thresholds:** Use a 1px `outline_variant` at 20% opacity *only* if it is paired with a `primary` glow effect to simulate a laser-cut edge.

### Surface Hierarchy & Nesting
Treat the UI as a series of stacked, semi-transparent polycarbonate sheets.
- **Base Level:** `surface` (#0E0E0F) - The deep obsidian void.
- **Component Level:** `surface_container` - The primary interactive plane.
- **Focus Level:** `surface_container_highest` - For active modals or high-priority alerts.

### The "Glass & Gradient" Rule
To achieve "The Kinetic HUD" look, all floating panels must use **Glassmorphism**:
- **Background:** `surface_variant` at 60% opacity.
- **Effect:** `backdrop-blur` (20px to 40px).
- **Signature Texture:** Primary CTAs must use a linear gradient from `primary` (#96F8FF) to `primary_container` (#00F1FD) at a 135-degree angle to provide "visual soul."

---

## 3. Typography
The typography system is a tension between technical precision and human readability.

- **Display & Headline (`Space Grotesk`):** This is our "Command" font. It is wide, technical, and aggressive. Use `display-lg` for hero moments, allowing characters to feel "architectural." Use tight letter-spacing (-0.02em) for headlines.
- **Body (`Inter`):** Our "Data" font. Clean and neutral to ensure maximum legibility against dark, high-contrast backgrounds.
- **Labels (`Manrope`):** Used for micro-copy and technical metadata. These should often be set in uppercase with increased letter-spacing (+0.05em) to mimic terminal readouts.

---

## 4. Elevation & Depth
In a cyberpunk world, depth is created by light and opacity, not physical shadows.

- **Tonal Layering:** Achieve "lift" by nesting `surface_container_lowest` cards inside a `surface_container_low` section. This creates a recessed, "slotted" appearance.
- **Ambient Glows:** Replace traditional grey drop shadows with **Luminous Halos**. Use the `primary` or `secondary` token at 10% opacity with a 32px blur to simulate the glow of a neon light against the obsidian background.
- **The "Ghost Border" Fallback:** If accessibility requires a container boundary, use `outline_variant` at 15% opacity. It should feel like a faint reflection on glass, not a structural box.

---

## 5. Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Gradient fill (`primary` to `primary_container`). Sharp 0px corners. Text is `on_primary`. Apply a `primary_dim` outer glow on hover/active states.
- **Secondary:** Transparent background with a `secondary` 1px "Ghost Border." Text is `secondary`.
- **Tertiary:** `tertiary` text with an underline that only spans 50% of the text width, aligned to the right.

### Input Fields
- **Styling:** `surface_container_high` background. No bottom border; instead, use a 2px vertical "accent bar" of `primary` on the left edge to indicate focus.
- **States:** Error states use `error` (#FF716C) with a subtle `error_container` glow.

### Cards & Lists
- **The Divider Rule:** Forbid the use of divider lines. Separate list items using `spacing-4` (1.4rem) or by toggling the background between `surface_container` and `surface_container_low`.
- **Interactive States:** On touch/press, the card background should flash to `surface_bright` momentarily, providing haptic-like visual feedback.

### Data Fragments (Custom Component)
Small, non-interactive chips used for metadata. Use `label-sm`, `tertiary` color, and a `surface_variant` background with a 45-degree clipped corner (achieved via CSS mask or clip-path).

---

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- **Do** use `0px` border-radius for everything. The system is aggressive and precise.
- **Do** leverage Fitts's Law by making all mobile touch targets at least 48dp in height, even if the visual element (like a text link) appears smaller.
- **Do** use progressive disclosure. Hide technical details behind "Expand Data" triggers to prevent cognitive overload.

### Don't
- **Don't** use soft, rounded corners. It breaks the "high-energy" technical aesthetic.
- **Don't** use standard grey shadows. Shadows must be obsidian (#000000) or tinted with the accent glow.
- **Don't** use 100% opaque borders. They flatten the "Glassmorphism" effect and make the UI feel like a 2010-era template.
- **Don't** use more than three accent colors on a single screen. Stick to a "Primary + One Accent" rule for 90% of the experience.
