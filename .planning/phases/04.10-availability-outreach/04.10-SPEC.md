# SPEC: Availability & Outreach (Booking Requests) — two-sided

**Feature:** Help DJs find gigs and promoters find DJs. Both sides record what they have open (DJ: dates + city; promoter: event slots), the system matches by city (+ genres on the promoter side) against each side's own contact book, and sends a personalized outreach email via Plunk from a customizable template. The DJ side attaches the EPK PDF to the request.
**Editions:** DJ app (OSS, self-hosted, single-user) — direction DJ→promoter. Promoter app (multi-tenant) — direction promoter→DJ, landing in the P4 Bookings area.
**Status:** Draft v2.1 for review — supersedes v1 (DJ-side-only). Reflects decisions: Wormhole dropped; EmailService extracted to an independent shared service; city-name matching confirmed; both directions in scope. v2.1 folds in the code-verified review findings in §15. **Q1 and Q3 resolved 2026-10-15 (§14): new `talent_contacts` table; new `talent.read`/`talent.write` authz actions.**
**Date:** 2026-10-15
**Branch:** `feat/availability-outreach`

---

## 1. Goal & Non-Goals

**Goal.** Two symmetric outreach flows on top of one shared email platform:

1. **DJ→promoter (DJ app).** The DJ records open dates/cities, sees which of their own promoter contacts match, and sends a personalized booking-request email with their EPK PDF attached, from a customizable template. Every send is logged; opt-outs are honored.
2. **Promoter→DJ (promoter app).** The promoter records open slots on their events (role/genres/fee), sees which of their own talent contacts (DJs they know) match by city + genre, and sends a personalized booking-offer email from a customizable template. Logged; opt-outs honored.

**Non-goals (this slice):**
- Cross-tenant / two-sided marketplace, public DJ directory, opt-in matching across KlubHub users (Cloud slice, deferred).
- Wormhole / public tunnel from a laptop (dropped — conflicts with SECURITY.md threat model and sealed-PII design).
- Geo-radius matching (no lat/lng on venues/contacts/talent; matching is by city name, confirmed).
- Bulk/campaign sends; auto-attaching the EPK to many recipients; auto-send of any kind.
- In-app DJ↔promoter messaging (email only, via Plunk).
- Contracts / e-signature / advancing (rest of P4 Bookings, separate).
- Per-org sender identity on the promoter side (v1 = instance-level sender; see §14).

## 3. Requirements

### 3.1 Shared email platform (EMAIL)

- **EMAIL-01** — Extract the transport-agnostic email primitives from `api/internal/finance` into **`api/internal/platform/email`**: `EmailMessage`, `EmailStatus`, `CreateEmailRequest`, `EmailAttachment`, `AttachmentSource` (interface over a minimal `AttachmentDoc`), `EmailSender`, `PlunkSender`, `DefaultSMTPSender`, `EmailService` (`Enqueue`/`Send`/`SendOwned`/`RetryFailed` + delivery worker), `EmailRepository`, and a `Render(subject, body string, vars map[string]string)` variable-substitution helper. `finance` keeps `InvoiceMailer` and its handlers and depends on `platform/email`.
- **EMAIL-02** — Extract the document-pointer layer (`Document`, `DocumentRepository`, `DocumentService`, `StorageAdapter`) from `finance` into **`api/internal/platform/docstore`**. `EmailService.WithAttachments` takes a `docstore`-backed `AttachmentSource`; `finance` and `outreach` both use it. The `documents` table itself is unchanged (platform migration 015).
- **EMAIL-03** — `EmailKind` moves to `platform/email` as an open registry: the type + `IsValid()` live there; each consumer registers its kinds (`finance`: `invoice_*`, `agreement_*`; outreach DJ: `booking_request`; outreach promoter: `booking_offer`). Unknown kinds are rejected at enqueue.
- **EMAIL-04** — Both binaries construct and wire an `EmailService`. The promoter binary gains Plunk config (§6.6) and starts the delivery worker (§6.8). The DJ binary's existing wiring (`api/cmd/api/main.go:468-496`) is re-pointed at the shared package.

### 3.2 Direction 1 — DJ → promoter (DJ app, `AVAIL` / `OUTR`)

- **AVAIL-01** — DJ can create / list / delete availability slots (date + city + genres + fee range + notes).
- **AVAIL-02** — For each slot, the system lists matching promoter contacts (`contacts.type = 'promoter'`, `city` match), ranked.
- **AVAIL-03** — Matching is case-insensitive on city; venues in the same city are surfaced as context.
- **OUTR-01** — DJ can CRUD outreach templates (name, subject, body); one default `dj_to_promoter` template is seeded.
- **OUTR-02** — Templates support variables (§7) resolved at preview and send.
- **OUTR-03** — DJ can send a booking request to a matched contact: pick a template (or write ad-hoc), optionally override subject/body, optionally attach an EPK PDF (chosen from EPK export history), send via Plunk.
- **OUTR-04** — The sent request is logged (contact, slot, template, EPK export, to, subject, body, status, sent_at) and linked to the `email_messages` row for delivery status.
- **OUTR-05** — A suppression (do-not-contact) list keyed by normalized email; sending to a suppressed address is refused; a contact can be marked do-not-contact (convenience endpoint that writes the suppression for that contact's email; there is no flag on `contacts`). Contacts with no email are returned by matching with `has_email=false` and cannot be sent to.
- **OUTR-09** — Slot validation: `date` must not be in the past; `UNIQUE (date, city, country)` (not just date+city, so two same-named cities in different countries do not collide).
- **OUTR-06** — The default template includes an opt-out footer; sender identity is the DJ's configured `PLUNK_FROM_EMAIL`/`PLUNK_FROM_NAME`; subject is non-deceptive. **Replies go to the DJ:** every request sets `ReplyTo` to the DJ's contact email (the `email_messages.reply_to` column from migration 032 and `PlunkSender` `reply` already support it); send is refused with 422 if no DJ contact email is configured, since the platform `From` address is not a mailbox the promoter's reply should reach.
- **OUTR-07** — Outreach sends are rate-limited (per-day cap) and only sent on explicit per-request action (no bulk auto-send, no auto-attach).
- **OUTR-08** — Frontend: an Outreach page (availability + matches + composer + sent log) and a template editor, with dev mock handlers.

### 3.3 Direction 2 — promoter → DJ (promoter app, `TALENT` / `OSLOT` / `OFFER`)

- **TALENT-01** — Promoter can CRUD **talent contacts** (DJs/artists they know): name, email, phone, city, country, genres, source (`past_gig` / `referral` / `manual`), notes. Name/email/phone are sealed with the tenant DEK + blind-indexed (audience pattern); city/country/genres stay plaintext as matching keys (same as DJ-side contacts).
- **TALENT-02** — One talent contact per email per tenant (blind-index unique); list/search filterable by city and genre.
- **TALENT-03** — "Mark do-not-contact" on a talent contact writes a suppression (by email blind index).
- **OSLOT-01** — Promoter can CRUD **open slots** on their events: role (e.g. "headliner", "warm-up"), genres, fee range + currency, notes. An event can have multiple open slots.
- **OSLOT-02** — For each open slot, the system lists matching talent contacts (city match + genre overlap), ranked, excluding suppressed.
- **OFFER-01** — Promoter can CRUD outreach templates (direction `promoter_to_dj`); one default template is seeded.
- **OFFER-02** — Templates support variables (§7) resolved at preview and send.
- **OFFER-03** — Promoter can send a booking offer to a matched talent contact: pick a template (or write ad-hoc), optionally override subject/body, send via Plunk (kind `booking_offer`). No attachment in v1 — event details go in the body.
- **OFFER-04** — The sent offer is logged (tenant, event, slot, talent contact, template, to, subject, body, status, sent_at) and linked to the tenant-scoped `email_messages` row.
- **OFFER-05** — A suppression list keyed by email blind index; sending to a suppressed address is refused.
- **OFFER-06** — The default template includes an opt-out footer; sender identity is the instance `PLUNK_FROM_EMAIL`/`PLUNK_FROM_NAME`; subject is non-deceptive. `ReplyTo` is the sending member's (or org's) contact email so DJ replies reach the promoter; refused with 422 if none is set.
- **OFFER-09** — **Location privacy.** `{{venue}}` renders empty (and `{{city}}` only) for events with `location_mode` `secret` before `location_reveal_at`, and empty for `city_only`; the composer shows a warning when a template uses `{{venue}}` on such an event. The offer must never disclose a venue the event withholds from the public.
- **OFFER-10** — Talent contacts without an email can be listed and matched but not sent to (`has_email=false` in match results; send returns 422).
- **OFFER-07** — Offers are rate-limited per tenant per day and only sent on explicit per-request action.
- **OFFER-08** — Frontend: the `bookings.vue` placeholder becomes a real Bookings page — open slots + matches + composer + sent log + talent list + template editor.

## 4. Shared Foundation: `internal/platform/email` Extraction

**Why:** the user requirement — EmailService is an independent service usable by finance, marketing, outreach, etc. Today the primitives live in `api/internal/finance` (`email_model.go`, `plunk_sender.go`, `invoice_email.go` uses them) and are mounted only in the DJ binary.

**Move to `api/internal/platform/email`** (package name `email`):

| From `finance` | To `platform/email` | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `EmailKind`, `validEmailKinds`, `IsValid` | `kind.go` | Open registry: `RegisterKind(EmailKind)`; finance + outreach register in `init()` |
| `EmailStatus`, `EmailMessage`, `CreateEmailRequest`, `EmailAttachment` | `model.go` | Unchanged shapes |
| `AttachmentSource` | `attachment.go` | Interface re-typed over `AttachmentDoc` (minimal: ID, OwnerType, OwnerID, Filename, MimeType) so consumers don't import finance |
| `EmailSender`, `PlunkSender`, `DefaultSMTPSender`, `PlunkConfig`, `SMTPConfig` | `sender.go`, `plunk.go`, `smtp.go` | Unchanged |
| `EmailService` (Enqueue/Send/SendOwned/loadAttachments/RetryFailed/worker) | `service.go`, `worker.go` | Unchanged semantics; `WithAttachments(docstore.Source)` |
| `EmailRepository` (+ `EmailRepositoryIface`) | `repository.go` | Gains optional tenant scoping (§6.8) |
| — (new) | `render.go` | `Render(subject, body string, vars map[string]string)` — `strings.NewReplacer` over `{{var}}` keys; shared by both outreach directions |

**Move to `api/internal/platform/docstore`:** `Document`, `DocumentOwnerType`, `DocumentKind`, `DocumentRepository`, `DocumentService` (register / get / download / list-by-owner / versioning), `StorageAdapter` (Garage S3). `finance` keeps `DocumentOwnerInvoice`/`DocumentKindInvoicePDF` constants and the invoice archive flow, now expressed over `docstore`.

**Finance after extraction:** `invoice_email.go` (`InvoiceMailer`) and the finance email handler stay in `finance`, importing `platform/email` + `platform/docstore`. No behavior change; existing finance tests keep passing against the moved code (imports updated).

**New kinds:** `booking_request` (DJ→promoter), `booking_offer` (promoter→DJ). `OwnerType` values: `outreach_request` (DJ side), `booking_offer` (promoter side); with EPK attached the message owner is the EPK export (§5.4).

### 5.4 EPK attachment (the one non-trivial integration)

EPK exports live in `epk_exports` with `garage_object_key` (migration 025) and do **not** register `documents` rows today. The attach path reuses the existing ownership-guarded machinery instead of a bespoke source:

1. Migration 034 adds `'epk_pdf'` to the `documents.kind` CHECK (`owner_type='epk'` is already allowed, 015).
2. At send time, the service upserts a pointer row: `documents(owner_type='epk', owner_id=<epk_export_id>, kind='epk_pdf', storage_key=epk_exports.garage_object_key, filename, mime_type='application/pdf', size_bytes, checksum, is_current=true)` — idempotent via the existing `documents_owner_kind_current_idx` unique index (reuse the current row if present; the bytes stay in Garage, never duplicated).
3. The message is sent with `SendOwned(OwnerType='epk', OwnerID=<epk_export_id>, AttachmentIDs=[docID])`, so `checkAttachmentOwner` passes at enqueue **and** at delivery retry (`loadAttachments` re-checks and downloads via `docstore` + Garage).
3a. `documents` requires `checksum_sha256`, `size_bytes` and `version`, and the immutability trigger (031) forbids changing them later, so the pointer row is created from the object's HEAD/bytes at first attach; if `epk_exports.garage_object_key` is empty (legacy `minio_path` rows pre-025) the send is refused. If a *re-exported* EPK reuses the same object key with different bytes, the stored checksum no longer matches — exports must write a new key per export (verify in `epk` before relying on this).
4. `outreach_requests` keeps `epk_export_id` + `email_message_id` for the log; the message's owner points at the EPK export (the entity the attachment belongs to). Without an EPK, the message owner is `outreach_request`/`<request id>` and no attachment is attached.

`PlunkSender` already supports base64 attachments (≤10 files / 10 MB; `maxEmailAttachmentBytes` = 7 MB content cap) — an EPK PDF is well within this.

### 6.1 Data model (promoter migrations 00014–00017; next free after `00013_audience.sql`)

All tables: `tenant_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES organizations(id) ON DELETE CASCADE`, `ENABLE` + `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY`, `tenant_isolation` policy on `current_setting('app.tenant_id')`, `GRANT` to `klubhub_app`, `data_class` comments — the exact pattern of `00013_audience.sql`. All service calls run inside `tenantdb.WithTenant`.

**00014_email_messages.sql** — tenant-scoped mirror of platform `017_email.sql` (+ `032` `reply_to`) with `tenant_id` + RLS. The promoter DB runs only `promoter/migrations`, so it needs its own copy; the shared `platform/email` repository serves both (tenant-scoped variant, §6.8).

**Not a column-for-column copy.** Platform `email_messages` stores `to_email`, `cc/bcc_emails`, `subject`, `body`, `body_html` and `reply_to` in plaintext. On the promoter side `to_email` is a talent contact's email and the rendered subject/body carry their name — copying the columns would put sealed PII in clear text in the outbox and make §6.4's "suppressions/log never store the email" false. So the tenant variant stores `to_email_enc`, `to_email_bidx`, `subject_enc`, `body_enc`, `body_html_enc`, `reply_to_enc` (all BYTEA, sealed with the tenant DEK, `Field{Table:"email_messages",…}`), no cc/bcc (not used by outreach), and carries `data_class` comments. The shared repository takes a `FieldCodec` (identity on the DJ side; envelope-backed on the promoter side) so the same service seals on write and unseals for delivery/log.

**Shared mechanics:** `email.Render` (`strings.NewReplacer` over `{{var}}`); unknown variables render as empty. Values are untrusted (contact names, notes): `Render` strips CR/LF from anything landing in the subject (header injection), and any HTML variant must HTML-escape values; v1 sends `text/plain` only. `{{venue}}` follows OFFER-09. Per-request override: the composer edits subject/body before sending; the edited copy is stored on the request row — the template is never mutated (rider RIDER-02 "per-gig copy, not a live link" precedent). Both default templates end with an opt-out footer ("If you'd rather not hear from us, reply 'stop' and we'll remove you.").

## 8. Matching Logic

**DJ→promoter (per availability slot):** `contacts` where `type='promoter'` AND `lower(trim(city)) = lower(trim(slot.city))` AND `deleted_at IS NULL`; exclude contacts whose normalized email is in `outreach_suppressions`; surface `venues` in the same city as context. Ranking: contacts with a past gig in that city rank higher; then alphabetical. Note `gigs` has no promoter-contact FK (only plaintext `promoter_email`/`promoter_name`, plus `gig_reader_contact_id`), so the join key is normalized `gigs.promoter_email = contacts.email`; contacts without an email cannot receive this boost. No fuzzy matching in v1 (noted limitation).

**Promoter→DJ (per open slot):** `talent_contacts` where `lower(trim(city)) = lower(trim(event.city))`; if both the event and the contact have genres, require overlap (`event.genres && talent.genres`); if either side is empty, city alone matches. Exclude suppressed (`email_bidx` match). Ranking: genre-overlap count desc, then most recently added. (No booking history exists yet — P4 bookings tables don't exist; when they do, "worked together before" becomes the top rank signal.)

Both: case-insensitive, trimmed city comparison. Geo radius is deferred until lat/lng exist.

## 9. Compliance & Consent

- **Lawful basis:** both directions are **B2B outreach to work emails under legitimate interest** (GDPR Recital 47), supported by relevance (the city/genre match) and a documented LIA. **Germany (§7 UWG) is stricter** and may require consent — flagged for legal review; the opt-out footer mitigates but does not replace consent where required.
- **Data provenance (the key strength):** both sides only ever email contacts from the sender's **own** book — the DJ's `contacts`, the promoter's `talent_contacts` (source field records how the relationship arose: past gig, referral, manual). Never scraped or purchased lists. The promoter side deliberately does **not** reuse the consent-based `audience_contacts` (that is the promoter's marketing audience — fans — a different purpose and lawful basis; see §14 Q1).
- **Sender identity:** from = configured `PLUNK_FROM_EMAIL`/`PLUNK_FROM_NAME` (the sender's real identity; on the promoter side the instance identity, §14 Q2). Never spoofed; non-deceptive subject.
- **Opt-out:** default-template footer + suppression list on both sides; sending to a suppressed address is refused (DJ: normalized email; promoter: blind index). Promoter-side suppressions never store the email itself. (Future: Plunk bounce/complaint webhook → auto-suppress.)
- **EPK attachment:** the EPK is the DJ's own press kit; the explicit "send" click is consent to share it with that one promoter. Only attach on an explicit per-request send — never auto-attach, never bulk.
- **Rate limiting:** reuse `RATE_LIMIT_RPM` (PROD-08) plus a per-day cap (`OUTREACH_MAX_PER_DAY`, default 50; per tenant on the promoter side). The cap counts `outreach_requests` created since local-midnight in the sender's timezone (failed attempts count, so retries cannot bypass it) and is checked-and-inserted in one transaction (advisory lock per tenant) so two concurrent sends cannot both pass at 49/50.
- **Opt-out must have an owner.** "Reply 'stop'" only works if a human acts on replies. Replies land in the sender's own inbox (Reply-To above); the UI shows a one-click "Do not contact" next to each sent request and a reminder in the footer docs. Inbound-reply/unsubscribe-link handling is future work (§14), not a v1 promise.
- **Unauthenticated DJ API.** The OSS DJ app has no user auth (CLAUDE.md); anyone who can reach `/api/v1/outreach/requests` can send mail from the DJ's Plunk identity with the EPK attached. The per-day cap bounds the damage; document in `SECURITY.md`/`SELF-HOSTING.md` that outreach must sit behind the operator's reverse-proxy auth, exactly as finance email already must.
- **Provider terms.** Confirm Plunk's acceptable-use terms allow person-to-person B2B outreach through the transactional endpoint (it is currently used only for invoices/agreements) before shipping; if not, a separate sender/stream is needed.
- **Sealed PII (promoter):** talent contact PII is sealed at rest; the suppression list stores only a blind index; the outreach log seals `to_email`.
