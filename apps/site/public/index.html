02 / EDITIONS

FREE TO RUN YOURSELF.
HOSTED WHEN YOU WANT IT.

KlubHub DJ is built to be owned. The self-hosted edition runs on your own machine or server with your own database and storage, and its source is MIT-licensed.

The rule we build by: your own data, and anything the law asks of you, like e-invoicing, is free. What costs real money to run, like servers, shared accounts and the approvals social platforms require, is where paid editions come in.

INSTALL IN ONE LINE

curl -fsSL https://klubhub.io/install.sh | bash

Needs Docker. It checks your machine, asks which KlubHub to run, creates every secret, starts it and prints the address. Re-run it any time to update; your data and keys are kept. KlubHub DJ runs on Apple Silicon Macs and ARM Linux servers such as a Raspberry Pi 5.
